## 3️⃣ Transport Inside Switzerland  

- **Swiss Travel Pass (3‑day, 2‑class)** – ₹30,000 per person. Covers unlimited travel on most trains, buses, boats, and mountain railways (e.g., Rigi, Pilatus, Jungfrau).  
- **Supplementary mountain tickets** (e.g., Jungfraujoch “Top of Europe”, Gornergrat) are **not** included – budget ₹15,000–20,000 each.  
- **Airport transfers** – Take the **train** from Zurich Airport to Zurich HB (≈ ₹1,200) or a shared shuttle (≈ ₹2,500).

## 5️⃣ Day‑by‑Day Plan  

### **Day 0 – Arrival**
- **Evening:** Land at Zurich Airport, take the S-Bahn to Hotel Adler. Light dinner at **Zeughauskeller** (Swiss classics).  

### **Day 1 – Zurich City Highlights**
- **Morning:** Walk the **Old Town (Altstadt)** – Bahnhofstrasse, Fraumünster (Chagall windows).  
- **Mid‑day:** Lunch at **Haus Hiltl** (world’s oldest vegetarian restaurant).  
- **Afternoon:** Visit **Kunsthaus Zurich** (art museum) + **Lake Zurich boat ride** (included with Travel Pass).  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Swiss Chuchi** (fondue).  

### **Day 2 – Day Trip to Rhine Falls & Stein am Rhein**
- **Train:** Zurich → Schaffhausen (≈ 30 min).  
- **Rhine Falls** – Europe’s largest waterfall (boat ride to the rock).  
- **Stein am Rhein** – Picture‑perfect medieval town.  
- **Return** to Zurich for night.  

### **Day 3 – Transfer to Interlaken & Lake Thun**
- **Morning:** Check‑out, take **InterCity** to Interlaken Ost (≈ 2 h).  
- **Afternoon:** Check‑in at Hotel Bernerhof, explore **Höheweg promenade** and **Harder Kulm** (funicular, 30 min).  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Restaurant Taverne** (local trout).  

### **Day 4 – Jungfraujoch – “Top of Europe”**
- **Early start:** Train Interlaken Ost → Lauterbrunnen → Kleine Scheidegg → Jungfraujoch (≈ 2 h 30 min).  
- **Activities:** Ice Palace, Sphinx Observatory, snow‑fun.  
- **Return** via the same route.  
- **Night:** Relax at the hotel; optional nightcap at **Hooters Interlaken** (casual).  

### **Day 5 – Lauterbrunnen Valley & Mürren**
- **Morning:** Train to **Lauterbrunnen** – walk to **Staubbach Falls**.  
- **Cable car** to **Mürren** (car‑free Alpine village).  
- **Hiking** (e.g., Allmendhubel Panorama Trail – 2 h).  
- **Afternoon:** Return to Interlaken, free time for shopping or a **paragliding** experience (≈ ₹12,000 per person).  

### **Day 6 – Transfer to Lucerne & Mt. Pilatus**
- **Morning:** Train Interlaken → Lucerne (≈ 2 h).  
- **Check‑in:** Ameron Luzern Hotel Flora.  
- **Mid‑day:** Take the **Golden Round‑Trip** – boat to Alpnachstad → world’s steepest cogwheel railway to **Pilatus** (included with Travel Pass).  
- **Evening:** Stroll the **Chapel Bridge**, dinner at **Wirtshaus Galliker** (traditional Lucerne fare).  

### **Day 7 – Lucerne & Mt. Rigi (Day‑trip)**
- **Morning:** Train to **Vitznau**, cogwheel to **Rigi Kulm** (“Queen of the Mountains”).  
- **Panoramic views** over four lakes; light hike.  
- **Afternoon:** Return to Lucerne, free time for souvenir shopping.  
- **Farewell dinner** at **Restaurant Balances** (lake‑front, fine dining).  

### **Day 8 – Departure**
- **Morning:** Check‑out, take the direct train from Lucerne to Zurich Airport (≈ 1 h).  
- **Fly home** with memories (and a well‑filled photo album!).

## 7️⃣ Money‑Saving Tips  

1. **Book Flights & Hotels together** on platforms like **Expedia** or **MakeMyTrip** – you often get a bundled discount of up to 10 %.  
2. **Travel in shoulder season** (late April‑early June or September‑October). Prices for accommodation drop 15‑20 % and crowds are thinner.  
3. **Use the Swiss Travel Pass** for all regular trains, buses, and boats; only pay extra for the three “premium” mountain excursions.  
4. **Eat like a local:** Grab lunch at **Bäckerei** (bakery) or **Schnitzel‑Stube**; a meal can be as low as ₹900.  
5. **Free attractions:** Zurich’s **Uetliberg** hike, Lucerne’s **Lake promenade**, and the **Alpine meadows** are all free.  
6. **Cash vs Card:** Switzerland is largely card‑friendly, but keep a small amount of CHF for remote mountain villages.
