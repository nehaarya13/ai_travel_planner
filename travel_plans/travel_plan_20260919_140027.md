## DETAILED ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo**  
1. **Immigration & Luggage** – Use the **Narita Express (N’EX)** or **Keikyu Line** (Haneda) to reach central Tokyo (≈¥ 3,000).  
2. **Hotel check‑in** – Recommended budget‑friendly 3‑star options (≈¥ 10,000/night):  
   * **APA Hotel & Resort Ryogoku Ekimae Tower** – 3‑star, 4.2 /5 on Booking.com, 5‑min from Ryogoku Station.  
   * **Hotel Mystays Asakusa‑Bashi** – clean, modern, close to Asakusa.  
3. **Explore Asakusa** – Senso‑ji Temple, Nakamise‑shopping street (souvenirs, snacks).  
4. **Dinner** – Try a local ramen shop in Ueno (≈¥ 900).  

**Cost Day 1**: Transport ¥ 3,000 + Hotel ¥ 10,000 + Meals ¥ 2,500 = **≈ ₹ 5,500**  

---

### **Day 2 – Classic Tokyo**  
*Morning* – **Tsukiji Outer Market**: Fresh sushi breakfast (≈¥ 1,500).  
*Mid‑day* – Walk to **Hamarikyu Gardens** (admission ¥ 300). Grab a quick bite from a vendor.  
*Afternoon* – **Meiji Shrine** (free) → Harajuku’s Takeshita‑dori (window‑shopping).  
*Evening* – **Tokyo Tower** observation deck (¥ 1,200) or free view from **Roppongi Hills** (Tokyo City View).  

**Transport** – 24‑hr Tokyo Metro pass ¥ 800.  

**Cost Day 2**: Metro ¥ 800 + Food ¥ 2,500 + Attractions ¥ 1,500 = **≈ ₹ 4,500**  

---

### **Day 3 – Tokyo → Kyoto**  
- **Shinkansen**: Use **JR Pass** for a **Hikari** train (no surcharge). Travel time ≈2 h 15 m.  
- **Bento**: Purchase a station bento at Kyoto Station (≈¥ 1,200).  
- **Check‑in** – Budget hotels near Kyoto Station (≈¥ 9,000):  
  * **Hotel Gracery Kyoto Sanjo** – excellent location, 3‑star.  
- **Fushimi Inari** – iconic torii tunnel (free, open 24 h).  
- **Evening** – Stroll Gion, maybe spot a geisha; dinner at a local izakaya (≈¥ 1,500).  

**Cost Day 3**: Food ¥ 3,200 + Hotel ¥ 9,000 = **≈ ₹ 7,200**  

---

### **Day 4 – Kyoto Highlights**  
- **Kinkaku‑ji (Golden Pavilion)** – Admission ¥ 400.  
- **Nishiki Market** – Snack tour (¥ 1,200).  
- **Arashiyama** – Bamboo Grove (free), **Tenryū‑ji** (¥ 500).  
- **Evening** – Dinner in Pontocho Alley (affordable kaiseki set ≈¥ 2,000).  

**Transport** – Kyoto city bus day pass ¥ 600 (covers all sites).  

**Cost Day 4**: Transport ¥ 600 + Entrance ¥ 900 + Food ¥ 3,200 + Hotel ¥ 9,000 = **≈ ₹ 7,700**  

---

### **Day 5 – Kyoto → Osaka**  
- **Shinkansen**: 15‑minute ride (JR Pass).  
- **Hotel** – Budget 3‑star in Namba/Umeda (≈¥ 9,500):  
  * **Hotel Sunroute Osaka Namba** – clean, central.  
- **Morning** – Kuromon Ichiba Market: fresh seafood lunch (≈¥ 1,500).  
- **Afternoon** – **Osaka Castle** (admission ¥ 600 + park ¥ 200).  
- **Evening** – Dotonbori neon lights, try takoyaki (≈¥ 800).  

**Cost Day 5**: Food ¥ 3,000 + Entrance ¥ 800 + Hotel ¥ 9,500 = **≈ ₹ 7,300**  

---

### **Day 6 – Nara Day‑Trip + Osaka Night**  
- **JR Pass** → **Nara** (≈45 min).  
- **Todai‑ji Temple** – Daibutsu Hall admission ¥ 600.  
- **Nara Park** – free (feed friendly deer).  
- **Lunch** – Kakinoha‑zushi (pressed‑leaf sushi) ≈¥ 1,200.  
- Return to Osaka → **Umeda Sky Building** (Floating Garden, ¥ 1,500).  
- **Shinsekai** – kushikatsu dinner (≈¥ 1,000).  

**Cost Day 6**: Food ¥ 3,200 + Attractions ¥ 2,100 + Hotel ¥ 9,500 = **≈ ₹ 7,800**  

---

### **Day 7 – Return to Tokyo & Departure**  
- Early **Shinkansen** back to Tokyo (JR Pass).  
- **Breakfast** at Tokyo Station’s “Gransta” (≈¥ 900).  
- **Last‑minute shopping** – Akihabara electronics or Ginza department stores (budget ¥ 5,000).  
- **Transport to Airport** – Narita Express (¥ 3,000) or Keikyu (¥ 1,200) depending on flight.  

**Cost Day 7**: Food ¥ 1,500 + Shopping ¥ 5,000 + Airport transfer ¥ 3,000 = **≈ ₹ 5,500**

## 5️⃣ PRACTICAL TIPS & TOOLS  

| Topic | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| **Visa** | Indian tourists need a **Temporary Visitor Visa** (single‑entry, up to 90 days). Apply 2–3 weeks before departure via the Japanese Embassy/Consulate. Required docs: passport, flight itinerary, hotel confirmations, bank statement (> ₹ 1 Lakh), completed V‑Form. |
| **Money** | Bring **₹ 10,000–₹ 15,000** in cash (¥ 10,000) for initial expenses; most places accept **credit cards** (Visa/Mastercard). Withdraw yen from ATMs (7‑Eleven, Post Office) – fee ≈ ¥ 200 per withdrawal. |
| **SIM / Data** | Purchase a **pre‑paid eSIM** (e.g., **Airalo – Japan Unlimited** ¥ 2,980 for 30 days, 15 GB). Works on all major carriers. |
| **Transport Apps** | *Japan Official Travel App* (JR Pass activation, timetables), *HyperDia* (train routes), *Google Maps* (walking & transit), *Navitime* (English). |
| **Packing** | Light layers (early‑Oct can be 15‑22 °C), comfortable walking shoes, universal power adapter (Type A/B), reusable water bottle (free refill stations). |
| **Cultural etiquette** | Remove shoes when entering traditional inns/temples, speak softly on public transport, carry cash for small stalls. |
| **Travel insurance** | Basic plan covering medical, trip‑cancellation, and baggage (≈ ₹ 1,200). Look for “Japan‑specific COVID‑free” policies. |
| **Booking timeline** | **Flights** – 2‑3 months early (price alert). **JR Pass** – buy online **before** arrival (exchange voucher at any JR office). **Hotels** – 30‑45 days early for the rates shown. |
